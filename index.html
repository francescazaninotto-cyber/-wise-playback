Case Study 01 · Generative · Welltech / Yoga Go

Yoga Go: Why Users Quit Before They Even Start

Mixed-methods study with 54 churned users across three behavioural segments — AI-conducted interviews via Heard, in-depth 1:1s, and product analytics. Led to a +18% onboarding uplift and +12% 30-day retention.

Generative Mixed methods Welltech · Yoga Go Shipped
Case Study 02 · Evaluative · King / Candy Crush Soda

Candy Crush Soda: The Psychology of Spending

Qual study with 11 players exploring guilt, loss aversion, and impulse spending in Candy Crush Soda. Behavioural science framing shifted monetisation strategy and launched a new accessibility research strand.

Why Wise

What I bring to the Mitigation team.

A background in behavioural science, experience studying how people make financial decisions under pressure, and a research practice built on understanding the gap between what people say and what they actually do.

Understanding how people make financial decisions

The King study explored guilt, loss aversion, and what drives spending behaviour — including why people misrepresent it. That's the kind of research I find most interesting.

Research at pace, without cutting corners

The Yoga Go study combined 48 AI-conducted interviews and 6 in-depth 1:1s in one sprint — fast enough to be useful, rigorous enough to be trusted.

B2C and B2B, consumer and professional

At Dow Jones I ran research across WSJ's consumer product and its B2B professional tools — two very different audiences, different decision contexts, different stakes. That breadth matters.
