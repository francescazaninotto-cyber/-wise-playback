The work

20+ years asking why people do the things they do.

I'm a Staff / Principal UX Researcher with a PhD in Cognitive Psychology and Behavioural Science. I've led research at Dow Jones (WSJ, Barron's, MarketWatch), King (Candy Crush), and Welltech — across everything from subscription retention to spending psychology to fraud prevention.

My approach is mixed-methods, behavioural-science-informed, and genuinely cross-functional. I like problems where the data tells you what but not why. That's where research earns its place.

Right now I'm excited about Wise's Mitigation team — because financial decision-making under pressure is exactly the kind of research I've been doing for a decade, just in different contexts.
